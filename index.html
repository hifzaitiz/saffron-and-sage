Who we are

A small kitchen with a long memory

Saffron & Sage started as a six-table dhaba on Fort Road. Three generations later we still cook from the same hand-written notebook, only now there is a rooftop above it.

  • Meat butchered and marinated the same morning
  • No food colouring, ever — the orange comes from chilli
  • Vegetarian and vegan plates on every section of the menu
  • Family-size portions, because that is how we eat at home
